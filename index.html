Remembering Elizabeth Seyoum Tefera

October 25th 1968 - September 21st 2026

A Life of Love, Strength, and Dedication

About Elizabeth Tefera

Elizabeth Seyoum Tefera was born on October 25, 1968 or ዓርብ, ጥቅምት 15 1961 according to the Ethiopian calendar. She was born in Awash, Ethiopia her parents were Asha Mohammed and Seyoum Tefera. Elizabeth was the oldest of 6 Children, a fact she held dear to her heart and something she also exemplified through her leadership style.She acheived her Associates Degree in Computer Information Systems from Strayer University, while also raising 3 children. She went on to have a successful 10+ year career at Marriott where she found lots of fulfillment. Elizabeth then moved to Oregon in late 2024 where she was working on her goal to open her own busness. She tragically passed away shortly before her goal could come to fruition.

Her Life Journey

October 25, 1968

Elizabeth Seyoum Tefera was born in Awash, Ethiopia. Elizabeth attended a French international school called Lycée Guébré-Mariam located in Addis where she learned to speak french from a young age, something she was very proud of. It also provided plenty of oppurtunities for Elizabeth as she would frequent the Chemin De Fer railway station located in Addis.Elizabeths father was actually employed by the railway company in the past aswell.

1992

Along with her Husband Elizabeth travelled to the United States, there she was welcomed by countless family members and loved ones.

Education

Elizabeth was somebody who was always trying achieve more and more during her short life, and she never settled. All while raising three kids she earned her Associate Degree in Computer Information Systems. Elizabeth then continued to have 10+ year career at Marriott, where she achieved countless accolades and distinguishment awards. Elizabeth was in the process of setting up a business with the aim of providing care and housing for those who need additional supportive care and special assistance. The business was to be named after her predeceased daughter with the business being named "Gigi's care.""

Family

Elizabeth's parents were Asha Mohammed and Seyoum Tefera. She was the oldest of six kids. Elizabeths family was her pride and joy. She always prided herself on her connection to her family no matter how spread out they might be, Elizabeth would be on the phone for endless amount of time being in contact with her siblings, neices and nephews in places such as Italy, the Netherlands and back home in Ethiopia. One of Elizabeths favorite things to do would be to ask her kids what they would like for her to make, after a little of back and forth they would settle on an idea,but nevertheless she would make an amazing meal no matter what it was and her true enjoyment would only come after seeing her kids enjoy a home cooked meal made by their mom. This is a memory I hope to hold onto for the rest of my life as Elizabeths son. Elizabeth wanted to be able to say she endued all these hardships life threw at her and she pervailed and overcame them which she did, but her story is also one of sacrifice and what is means to love someone else or something else more than you love your self, that also was a sentiment she emodied. May she rest eternally in peace and power in heaven. .
